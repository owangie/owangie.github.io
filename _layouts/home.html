Data curator

{{ site.author.name }}

An art curator traces an object's story from its marks and records. I do the same with development data: I find the patterns and the storyline in the numbers, catch the early signals, and make results measurable. For the text, I build large language model (LLM) pipelines that turn thousands of project documents into structured, citable evidence.
