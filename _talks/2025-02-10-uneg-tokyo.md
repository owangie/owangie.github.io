Presented, with colleagues from the GCF Independent Evaluation Unit, on the IEU's experience with AI in evaluation:

* Using AI for large scale text mining of evaluation documents
* Working with large language models: how to reduce hallucination and get more accurate results
* Tools for harvesting evidence from surveys and questionnaires
* Using AI alongside theory of change

I also made a slide on the IEU LORTA impact evaluation of a deforestation project in Timor-Leste, to explain the idea of the counterfactual to the audience, and designed interactive Mentimeter slides so that academics, UN participants and think tanks could respond during the talk.
