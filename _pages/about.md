I am an Evaluation Associate at the Independent Evaluation Unit of the Green Climate Fund, where I work on portfolio analysis, evidence synthesis and applications of AI in evaluation.

Previously I was a Data Scientist at the United Nations Development Programme in New York, working on corporate results reporting, dashboards and text analysis of evaluation reports, with a detail assignment to the UN Office for South-South Cooperation. I have also worked with the World Food Programme and the UN Capital Development Fund.

I hold a Master of Public Policy from the University of Maryland, with a concentration in development economics, and a B.A. from Beijing Foreign Studies University.

Interests
======
* Results measurement and indicator quality in development finance
* Machine learning and large language models for analysing project documents
* Climate finance and portfolio performance
