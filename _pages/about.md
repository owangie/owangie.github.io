I build the evidence systems that tell development institutions whether their money is changing lives. For six years I have worked inside the results and evaluation functions of multilateral organisations, first at **UNDP** and now at the **Green Climate Fund's Independent Evaluation Unit**, writing the code, testing the indicators, and briefing the senior managers and Board members who act on the findings.

My work sits where data science meets development operations: large language model pipelines that read thousands of project documents, portfolio analytics across climate funds, and dashboards that country offices use every week.

What I bring to outcomes measurement
======

**Indicators and results frameworks**
* Read, code and quality assure results frameworks in funding proposals, progress reports and evaluation reports
* Sole quantitative analyst on the GCF [Evaluation of the Approach to Country Ownership](https://ieu.greenclimate.fund/document/finalreport-coa2025); authored the quantitative annex and econometric analysis

**AI for outcomes**
* Focal point for AI and advanced analytics in the GCF evaluation unit; represent GCF in the UNEG AI Working Group
* Built an LLM screening pipeline (Azure AI Foundry, GPT models) that classifies the full GCF funding proposal portfolio against evaluation criteria, with an audit trail for every decision

**Portfolio analytics and forecasting**
* Benchmarked disbursement performance across four climate funds (GCF, Adaptation Fund, CIF, GEF)
* Led portfolio and data analysis for regional, thematic and private sector evaluations

**Communicating to decision makers**
* Presented evaluation findings to Board members and senior management
* Drafted 300+ policy briefs from UN Security Council and interagency sessions for WFP senior management

**Building teams and capacity**
* Trained colleagues on responsible AI adoption; trained UNOSSC staff in data analysis and results monitoring
* Recruited and supervised three early career professionals, including a PhD researcher

Why practitioner experience matters
======

Outcome indicators fail in practice for practical reasons: a baseline no one collected, a results framework written to pass review, a dashboard nobody opens. I have seen each of these from the inside and built tools that fix them. I pair quantitative training (Master's in Public Policy, University of Maryland, development economics concentration) with production code that real teams use.
