The UN Office for South-South Cooperation manages several trust funds, each with its own board and reporting rules, including the Pérez-Guerrero Trust Fund and the India–UN Development Partnership Fund. When I joined in 2024 on an eight month detail assignment from UNDP, there was no dashboard bringing them together.

I built the UNOSSC Trust Fund Dashboard from scratch in Power BI: portfolio and donor contribution views for the funds, and a set of KPIs to track how each one was doing. I also trained six staff to run and use it, so it kept going after I left. It became part of the Office's monitoring system, and later reviews of the Office draw on it.
