When schools began closing in early 2020, millions of children lost the meal they relied on each school day. From WFP's New York office, I worked with the team at headquarters in Rome on WFP's *Global Monitoring of School Meals during COVID-19 School Closures*. My part was sharing data and early mapping ideas, worked up in Tableau, on where the need was greatest. The Rome team built and published the final map, updated daily with school closures and the number of children no longer receiving school meals, country by country. At launch it showed more than 364 million schoolchildren missing out ([WFP press release, March 2020](https://www.wfp.org/news/new-digital-map-shows-terrible-impact-covid-19-school-meals-around-world)).
