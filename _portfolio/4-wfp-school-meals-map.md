When schools began closing in early 2020, millions of children lost the meal they relied on each school day. From WFP's New York office, I worked with the team at headquarters in Rome to put together WFP's *Global Monitoring of School Meals during COVID-19 School Closures*: I shared data and ideas for mapping where the need was greatest, and built in Tableau. The result was a map updated daily with school closures and the number of children no longer receiving school meals, country by country. At launch it showed more than 364 million schoolchildren missing out ([WFP press release, March 2020](https://www.wfp.org/news/new-digital-map-shows-terrible-impact-covid-19-school-meals-around-world)).
